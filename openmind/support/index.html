Open a support request on GitHub. Requests are public and need a free GitHub account, so please don't include personal information.

What do the numbers mean?

OpenMind counts how often each AI model answered successfully, and how quickly, in OpenMind's own logged runs. It doesn't grade how good the answers were. Each number in the app shows its sample size, its date range and its source. In the app, see Leaderboard › How this is ranked, or Settings › About the data.

Why isn't a model ranked?

A model needs at least 10 logged runs before it's ranked. Below that, a rank would be guesswork, so it's listed under "Not ranked yet".

Why is there no price for a model?

OpenMind shows a price only when it can link the provider's own price page and the date it was checked. Otherwise it says "No sourced price yet" rather than guess.

The data looks old

The app shows the date the data is true as of. Pull down on the Leaderboard to check for new data. Offline, it shows the last copy it downloaded and says when.

Is OpenMind connected to the AI companies?

No. OpenMind is independent. Model and company names identify the models measured; OpenMind isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
