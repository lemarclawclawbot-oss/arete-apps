Help for Aretē Praxis on iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my progress?

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Reset all progress. This deletes your saved learning progress from your iPhone and cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is inside the app, including the 3D PC-build lab. The app makes no network requests, so airplane mode makes no difference.

What is the difference between Learn mode and Assessment mode?

Learn mode shows part labels, hints and live temperatures. It helps you practise but can never earn mastery. Assessment mode hides the labels and gives you a debrief only after you power on.

How does mastery work?

Praxis tracks what you have shown you can do for each skill. Your mistakes become targeted scenarios in a later session. You reach mastery by passing a transfer test on a case or board you have not seen, correct on the first power-on. Spaced retrieval checks come back later to confirm it stuck. A checklist item ticks only when the simulator verifies your placement is correct.

Is it an AI tutor?

No. There is no AI or generated content while you use it. The simulator follows fixed rules, so the same cable or build gives the same result every time.

Does it cover the CompTIA A+ exam?

Praxis is a hands-on practice tool for some hardware skills. It does not claim to cover any exam's objective list, and it cannot promise that you will pass any exam. It is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CompTIA, Inc.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18.0 or later, in portrait. There is no iPad version.

Does it cost anything or collect my data?

It is free, with no purchases, subscriptions or ads. It collects no data. See the privacy policy.

The 3D bench is blank or something looks wrong.

Close the app fully and open it again. If the problem continues, open a support request (see Contact below) with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Contact

Please open a support request on GitHub. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing.

Support requests on GitHub are public. Do not include your name, email address or any other personal details.
