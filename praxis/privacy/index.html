Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · Applies to the Aretē Praxis iOS app.

The short version

  • Aretē Praxis collects no data about you.
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
  • Your learning progress is stored only on your iPhone. You can delete it in Settings.

1. Who we are

Aretē makes Aretē Praxis. For any privacy question, open a support request on GitHub.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. We have no server for the app to send data to, and the app does not contact one.

3. What is stored on your iPhone

To remember where you are, the app stores on your device:

  • Your learning progress: which skills you have practised, your results, and when to review them. It is kept in the app's own storage area on your iPhone (Application Support/Praxis).
  • A single setting that remembers you have seen the introduction.

We cannot see any of this. It is not sent anywhere. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you use them. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

4. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings, then Reset all progress.
  • Or delete the app from your iPhone. This removes its stored data from the device.

5. The 3D PC-build lab

The PC-build lab uses a web view to draw a 3D bench. Every file it uses is inside the app. It does not load anything from the internet, blocks network requests, and keeps no web data (cookies, cache or storage) after it closes.

6. Third parties

Aretē Praxis uses no third-party services and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It includes the open-source three.js library (MIT licence), which runs locally on your device. There are no advertisers and no analytics providers.

7. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications.

8. Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either. The app has no accounts, chat, ads or purchases.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.

10. Contact

Please open a support request on GitHub.

Support requests on GitHub are public. Do not include your name, email address or any other personal details.
