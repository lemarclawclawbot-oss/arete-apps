NEXUS support

NEXUS is a private notes and memory app for iPhone. Everything you save stays on your iPhone unless you connect your own server.

Where are my notes stored?

On your iPhone, in NEXUS's private storage. There is no NEXUS account and no NEXUS cloud.

How do I back up or move my notes?

Settings › Export as Markdown creates a .zip with one Markdown file per memory. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or open it in any notes app.

How do I save something from Safari or another app?

Tap Share, then NEXUS. If NEXUS isn't in the row, tap More and switch it on. The item appears in your library the next time you open NEXUS.

How do I link notes?

Open a memory and tap Link to… to connect it to any other memory, or tap Suggest links and NEXUS compares tags and words on your iPhone to suggest related ones. Links appear on the memory and in the Graph tab.

Dictation says it isn't available

NEXUS only uses on-device speech recognition, so your words never leave your iPhone. If your language has no on-device model, use the microphone key on the iOS keyboard instead.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Erase all data, then confirm twice. This can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

What is "Connect your own NEXUS server"?

An optional setting for people who run their own NEXUS server. It accepts HTTPS addresses only, and your access key is kept in the iOS Keychain. It is off by default and you don't need it to use NEXUS.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include anything private.
