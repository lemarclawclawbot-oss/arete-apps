NEXUS privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to Aretē NEXUS for iPhone, version 1.0 and later.

Short version: NEXUS does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you write, capture, link or share into NEXUS is stored on your iPhone inside the app's private storage. The developer of NEXUS cannot see it.

Dictation

When you dictate a note, speech is turned into text on your iPhone. NEXUS does not use Apple's server-based speech recognition.

Link previews (optional, off by default)

If you turn on link previews in Settings, NEXUS downloads thumbnails from the website of a link you saved (for example YouTube). That website can see the request, as with any web page you open.

Your own server (optional, off by default)

If you connect a NEXUS server that you run, NEXUS sends what you capture, search and ask to that server over HTTPS, using the access key you enter. That server is operated by you, not by the developer of NEXUS. Its handling of your data, including any AI provider it uses, is governed by whoever runs it. The access key is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Sharing into NEXUS

The NEXUS share extension saves the link or text you share into a private folder on your iPhone that only NEXUS can read. It does not connect to the internet.

Export and deletion

Settings › Export as Markdown gives you a copy of all your memories. Settings › Erase all data permanently deletes them from your iPhone, along with drafts and any saved server key. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

NEXUS is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include anything private.
