What OmniProp is

A research and discipline tool for sports player props. You enter the props you are weighing, log evidence on both sides, and record your decisions. OmniProp grades your decision process and tracks how well your estimates hold up.

OmniProp never places, links to, or promotes a wager. It has no odds feed, no live data, no money, and no account.

Common questions

Where does the data come from? From you. The bundled demo league is fictional: its players, teams, statistics, and results are invented for practice.

Why can't I add a prop to my slate? A discipline gate is holding it. Open the prop: the "Discipline gates" section shows which check is not met yet (for example, the stat rule is unchecked, or availability is not confirmed).

Why does a slate show a warning? Two or more props depend on the same game or team, so they tend to succeed or fail together.

How is the process grade worked out? From five checks captured when you decide: evidence on both sides, the stat rule, availability, shared exposure, and your slate limit. The result never changes the grade.

Is my data backed up? Only in your own device or iCloud backup. OmniProp itself sends nothing anywhere.

How do I delete everything? Settings › Erase all my data, or delete the app.

Responsible play

OmniProp is for adults (18+) and is not advice. If gambling is causing you or someone you know harm, help is free and confidential:

  • US: National Problem Gambling Helpline, 1-800-522-4700, or ncpgambling.org.
  • Elsewhere: contact your local support service.
