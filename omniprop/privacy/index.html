Aretē OmniProp Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Short version: OmniProp collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What OmniProp stores, and where

OmniProp stores the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • the props you enter (player, team, game, stat, line, side, your estimate, your notes and evidence);
  • your slates, watch list, and hidden props;
  • your Ledger decisions, results, and reflections;
  • your slate-size limit, and whether you have seen the introduction.

This data never leaves your device through OmniProp. iOS may include it in your own device or iCloud backup, as it does for any app; OmniProp does not read or send those backups.

What OmniProp does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network connections. It has no servers, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell, share, or disclose any data, because it receives none.

Links that leave the app

Settings contains links to this policy, the support page, and the National Council on Problem Gambling (phone and website). Opening them hands you to Safari or the Phone app; those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all my data removes every prop, slate, and Ledger decision. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

OmniProp is rated 18+ and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy label will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open a support request. Please do not include personal information in a support request; requests are public.
